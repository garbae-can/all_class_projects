Garbage herself...

Garbage, otherwise known as garbae/garbaecan online, is a character I created back in 2021, originally based off a character I was using as my online persona.

photos/pregarbae.jpg

This character had no specific name and actually just went by Amari. She actually sparked the creation of N0NAME, but that’s a whole other story. This character was an extension of myself, still wanting to express myself online. After working on an AU, I wanted to make another version of this character but ended up liking the new version so much she became my main OC — and thus, Garbage was born! With a little bit of tweaking over the years, Garbage has had many colors and phases. The AU she was based off was later expanded and I made another version of Garbage for the AU.

Garbage's Eyes

Garbage's eyes change colors with the emotions she feels. Although her eyes are normally pink, they can turn red or into hearts depending on what she feels. When Garbage’s eyes become hearts, it means she has fallen in love or is feeling strong affection. This is rare—Garbage has a hard time falling in love—and her powers weaken when she does like someone. She may even struggle to use her powers on someone she deeply cares for.
